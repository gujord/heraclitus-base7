Checked mathematical content
Slide 9 was extracted and visually inspected. It states the conjectural
equivalence between coverage by the Heraclitus transform and an infinite
positive step set with gcd 1. The same slide expresses doubt about the
sufficiency of these conditions. The public English automatic captions,
approximately 09:30-10:15, agree on the two conditions and equivalence.
The manuscript paraphrases the mathematical statement; it does not quote
informal spoken wording. No independent audio transcription is claimed.
Powers of seven meet the two conditions because they form an infinite set
containing 1. This project's coverage argument addresses that particular
set and yields no conclusion for arbitrary admissible step sets.
